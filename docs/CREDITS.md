## Original work

All GDScript, shaders, procedural tree/rock/building geometry, the noise-based terrain and
hydrology, and the generated texture atlases (`tools/make_textures.py`: conifer needles, leaf
cluster, grass tuft, ripple normal map, waterfall streaks) were produced for this project and
are released with it under CC0.
